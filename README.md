# Retail-Store-Sales-Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Retail Store Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer purchasing behavior, and regional sales.

The dashboard provides key business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, slicers, and profit-and-loss analysis, helping users understand sales trends and identify top-performing states, customers, and product sub-categories.

## Project Objectives

Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity sold.

Monitor monthly profit and loss trends.

Compare sales performance across different states.

Identify top-performing customers and product sub-categories.

Understand customer preferences for product categories and payment methods.

Enable interactive filtering for better business analysis.

## Tools & Technologies

## Dashboard Features

1. Interactive Filters

Quarter Slicer: Filter sales data by Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4.

State Slicer: View sales performance for a selected state.

Reset Button: Clear all slicer selections and return to the default view. Hold Ctrl and click the button to reset the filters.

2. Sales and Profit Analysis

Total Amount by State: Compare sales across states and identify the top 4 performing states.

Total Profit by Month: Analyze monthly profitability and identify profit and loss periods.

Conditional Formatting: Use different colors to distinguish profitable and loss-making months.

3. Product Analysis

Quantity by Category: Understand the distribution of product quantities across Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture.

Profit by Sub-Category: Compare profitability across product sub-categories and identify top performers.

4. Customer Analysis

Top 4 Customers: Identify the customers contributing the highest sales amount.

5. Payment Mode Analysis

Quantity by Payment Mode: Analyze product quantities across different payment methods, including UPI, credit card, debit card, cash on delivery, and EMI.

## Key Insights

Maharashtra is the highest-performing state by sales among the states displayed.

Clothing contributes the largest share of total quantity sold among the three product categories.

Printers are the highest-performing sub-category by profit in the displayed analysis.

Monthly profit analysis highlights periods of both positive and negative profitability.

Customer and payment-mode visualizations help identify purchasing patterns and key contributors.
